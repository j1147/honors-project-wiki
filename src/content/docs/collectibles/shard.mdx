A **Shard** is the final goal of each section of the game. They are rumored by Castle Guy and Mousey to contain special powers, such as levitation and telekinesis. In the game, there are five in total. Upon collection, a splash text and particle affect appears, which is unique to Shards.

## Trivia
* Originally, there were going to be five parkour sections with associated Shards. In the final game, the last Shard was given to Doozy and must be won by a race.
* The Shards were originally going to unlock a level in the tower each, similar to how a Bridge Part unlocks a part of the bridge.
