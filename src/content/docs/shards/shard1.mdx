The **Strafe Shard** is the first shard to be encountered. Its associated course consists of three sections of moving platforms. The first section is 3 rotating platforms which drag the player with it, making timing jumps difficult. The second section is composed of interleaved moving platforms, some of which move vertically. The third section is most challenging, as it is composed of many fast moving platforms.

## Bridge Parts
 * At the end of section 1
 * At the end of section 2
 * Behind a rock underlying section 1


## Trivia
 * The Strafe Shard is the first shard to be encountered and is also the first shard to have been made
 * This is the only Shard to be contained within its pedestal
