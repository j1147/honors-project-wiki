The **Lunge Shard** is the second Shard to be encountered. It is a pink Shard, similar to a diamond. The course starts off with a new mechanic called the slimeball. Slimeballs launch the player to varying heights, with the highest jumps being delivered from the center, requiring very precise movements to avoid falling off one. The first slimeball launches the player to a long, thin platform with thick wooden pillars that occupy most of the walking space. The player must make "neo" style jumps around the pillars by jumping on the very edge of the platform. Following this, three more slimeball jumps lead to a set of more pillar jumps. On three three-tiles-wide platforms, pillars occupy 2 of the tiles on each one. Finally, a set of four slimeball jumps precludes the Shard.

## Trivia
* The Lunge Shard is the only course that is entirely in the air.
* Its course has the most checkpoints (3), as it has some of the most difficult jumps in the game due to the unpredictability of the slimeballs.
* The Lunge Shard also has the most Bridge Parts contained in and near its course.
