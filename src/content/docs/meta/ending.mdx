The **Ending** is the final sequence of the game. After completing the bridge and climbing the tower, the player finds Castle Guy, the twist enemy of the series. After a brief conversation, Castle Guy admits that the player was unknowningly collecting the shards for himself rather than to save the tower. It is unclear whether Mousey was aware of this. Using the power of the Shards, Castle Guy then launches the player into space, never to be seen again. It is implied that shortly afterward the island's tower and its inhabitants are obliterated.

## Trivia
 * The ending is very similar to the demo, except you get launched much further
 * Originally, it was planned for the tower to fall into the void as Castle Guy escapes
