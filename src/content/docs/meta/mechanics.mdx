There are several different mechanics present in the game besides moving platforms and basic jumps.

## Collectibles
Over the course of the game, the player must collect four Shards. Additionally, there are Bridge Parts scattered among the map which must be collected in order to physically access the ending. For more information, see Bridge Parts.

## Checkpoints and Dying
The player can occasionally die to obstacles, especially on the last two Shards. When this happens the player is sent back to the last touched checkpoint, or if the player has never touched one, to the beginning of the game.

## Slimeballs
Slimeballs are elliposidal bounce pads that can unpredictably launch the player into the air. They are one of the main gimmicks in the Lunge Shard. If not timed correctly, jumping on them may lead to the player being knocked off or launched high into the sky. They are suspected to be so unpredictable because the developer made the collision boxes too small.

## The Bridge
The Bridge unlocks the ending and require Bridge Parts to be built. When a player gets near a physical part of the bridge (not the collectible), it consumes one Bridge Part from the player's inventory and places itself in the world.
