The **Ending** is the final sequence of the game. After completing the bridge and climbing the tower, the player finds Castle Guy, the twist enemy of the series. After a brief conversation, Castle Guy admits that the player was unknowningly collecting the shards for himself rather than to save the tower. It is unclear whether [Mousey](/characters/mousey) was aware of this.

## Trivia
 * The ending is very similar to the demo, except you get launched much further
 * Originally, it was planned for the tower to fall into the void as Castle Guy escapes
