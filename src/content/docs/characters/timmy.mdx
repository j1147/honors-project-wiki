**Timmy** is the persona of the player. Their origins are unknown, but they are suspected to be from near Lockhart, Texas, due to the high incidence of aerial hot air balloon accidents. They unfortunately suffer from quadriplegia, but are still ambulatory due to unseen forces. Timmy was trapped on the island after an accident with their hot air balloon left them stranded on the southern side. At the end of the game, they are launched into space and presumed dead, though the ending is up to interpretation.


## Trivia
* Originally, the player played as a robot due to animation constraints on the game. This was changed last minute at the price of no animation.
